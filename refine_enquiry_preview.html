Case study · Desborough

A dated family bathroom and cloakroom, reset in warm stone.

Location Desborough, Northamptonshire
Timeframe 15 days on site
Investment £10,500

We reset both rooms around the same warm stone-effect tile so the two spaces read as part of the same house. In the main bathroom, a new P-shaped shower bath with a curved glass screen and a brushed steel rainfall shower replaced the old enclosure. Recessed lit niches, a fitted anthracite vanity and matching WC unit brought in the storage the room was missing.

"Nathan and the team were so clean and tidy throughout, and completely professional from start to finish. We absolutely love our new en-suite — the storage they built in has made such a difference."

Christine S — Desborough, Northamptonshire
